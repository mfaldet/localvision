Sources and limits

Where the figures come from

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 5-year estimates, 2015–2019 and 2020–2024, tables B01003 (population), B11001 (households), B19013 (median household income) and B25064 (median gross rent).

This site is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau. Anything you conclude from these figures is your own conclusion, not the Bureau's.

What to keep in mind

  • These are survey estimates, not counts. Each one has a margin of error that this page does not show. For small places and for census tracts the margin can be large, so treat small differences as no difference.
  • Each period covers five years of surveys. The two periods do not overlap, which is why they can be compared.
  • Income and rent for 2015–2019 are shown in 2024 dollars. They were multiplied by 1.2307, the factor the Census Bureau gives for this pair of periods.
  • Census tracts were redrawn in 2020, so tracts show 2020–2024 only.
  • Where the Bureau published no estimate, or only "$250,000 or more" or "$3,500 or more", the area is left blank.
  • The figures are saved with this page and refreshed once a year. Only the four measures above are available here.
  • The Census Bureau advises caution when comparing any period that includes 2020 with other periods.
